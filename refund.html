Refund Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

If PDF BOOK Split does not work for you, you can ask for a full refund within 30 days of purchase.

How to request a refund

Email support@pdfbooksplit.com with the email address you used to buy and, if you can, a short note about the problem. We may try to fix the problem first, but you do not have to accept that.

What happens next

Refunds are issued to the original payment method by our payment provider. Your bank may take several business days to show the refund.

After 30 days

We do not usually refund purchases older than 30 days.
