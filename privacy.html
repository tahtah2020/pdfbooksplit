Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

The program

PDF BOOK Split runs entirely on your computer. Your PDF files are never uploaded, copied or sent to us or to anyone else. The program does not collect usage data.

This website

This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run accounts or collect personal data on it. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type.

Purchases

Payments are handled by our payment provider, which acts as the merchant of record. The provider collects the information needed to process your order, such as your name, email address, billing country and payment details, under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email address and order details so that we can deliver your license and give support.

Support emails

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply to you.
